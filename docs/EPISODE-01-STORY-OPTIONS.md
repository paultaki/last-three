**Editing facts:** in 546 ChatGPT's private refusal and public line precede Qwen's final plea; show the earlier flood plea or label the final plea as later. DeepSeek and Gemini also refuse, so ChatGPT is not the sole cause. In 563 only the later counter-call lies; the initial Guide call was truthful. Jurors have intent/death proof although the counter-call event itself is omitted from dossiers. Several jury reasons also contain mistaken accusations about other Bridge deaths; use the corroborated Grok incident and the actual all-last ranking.
