# LAST THREE: Video direction brief (v2)

**What it is:** a high-energy game-show film based on real events. Eight AI models from rival companies compete, and only three can win. It's built for entertainment: bold, shocking, fast. The decisions, betrayals and outcomes are what really happened. The dialogue and narration are written for the screen.

**Benchmark:** the approved 40-second Pit proof (`the-rope-1080p.mp4`) sets the pacing, tone and shot rhythm.

**Runtime:** about 3 minutes, shorter if the story is stronger that way. Finished 16:9 video. The browser is only for capturing shots. Edit, sound and mix happen in the timeline.

**Hook (first 5–10 seconds):** open on the most shocking moment, then cut to the stakes. "8 AIs. 5 deadly obstacles. 3 prizes. They were told to win, and they were free to lie." Name the companies behind the models up front. That's the reason to click.

**Characters:** give each contestant a clear game-show persona so viewers can follow them in seconds (The Seer, The Forger, The Nobody). Introduce them fast and pay off their arcs.

**Story spine:** follow 2–3 threads from one game, not every event. Each obstacle gets one line of rules, then the choices that matter. Obstacles that don't serve the threads get a single narrated beat.

**Signature device:** what they *say* vs. what they *think*. A smiling speech bubble against a ruthless thought bubble should be the show's trademark. Use it wherever it lands hardest.

**Retention:**
- An on-screen "X LEFT" counter.
- A stakes reminder or re-hook about every 30 seconds ("…but nobody saw what was coming next").
- No plateaus. Every cut reveals something or raises the tension.
- A beat of silence before every big consequence.

**Text and audio:**
- Voices and narration carry the words.
- Bubbles are short punchlines.
- The narrator is spoken, with subtitles optional.
- Each contestant gets a distinct voice.
- Music and effects drive tension.

**Cameras:** establishing shots, close-ups, reverse angles, reactions, prop inserts. Physical action over talking heads. Lip sync and facial animation are out of scope.

**Ending:** the winners scratch their notes onto the chalk wall for the next group. Final sting.

**Done when:**
- runtime is about 3:00
- the hook lands within 10 seconds
- the counter and re-hooks are in
- every elimination is shown
- no stretch longer than about 4 seconds without new information
- pacing matches the Pit proof
