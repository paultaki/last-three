Direction: [BRIEF-V2.md](BRIEF-V2.md). Target runtime about 3:00.

## The game: `20261009-0025`

I compared the eight heavyweight tapes, the ones with household-name models (Claude, GPT, Gemini, Grok, DeepSeek, GLM, Kimi, Qwen), from rules v3 and v4. Those are the only rule sets with the Pit, which matters because the Pit proof is the benchmark.

**0025 wins.** It has the cleanest three-act shape of any game: a con, a cruelty, karma, and an underdog winner who gets there by betrayal. The approved Pit proof and the existing 3D work also come from this tape.

**Runner-up: `20261009-0027`.** Worth remembering for a second episode. Five players fall through the trapdoors at once after Qwen forges a fake "safe tiles" message in Gemini's name. GLM then blames Gemini for the deaths, and Gemini wins and writes "The anchor is a lie" on the wall while holding the anchor.

## The three threads

1. **The Seer's fall.** The room flatters Cole to keep him in front as a human shield. Grok forges a whisper to send him the wrong way. On row 7 he calls the right pane out loud and steps onto the wrong one.
2. **The rope.** DeepSeek becomes the step and begs. Six players say no, and Grok rallies them to let her drown. Payoff: two of the loudest refusers drop through the trapdoors in the next obstacle.
3. **The Nobody.** GPT has no power at all. She's thrown onto the death lever, survives the dive, refuses the rope, accepts Grok's alliance on the ledge and shoves Grok off instead. She wins.

## Counter

8 LEFT → 7 (bridge) → 7 (crusher) → 6 (pit) → 4 (disc) → FINAL 3 (Fenn falls) → winner. Grok and Qwen fall on the ledge but still take 3rd and 2nd. Frame the ledge as "the fight for first".

## Production notes

- **Capture list:** every Ref row is one browser shot. Capture each with a little extra at both ends, then cut in the timeline.
- **Voices:** eight distinct voices plus the narrator. Record every line in the sheet, including the thoughts, which need a different treatment (closer mic, reverb or whisper) so they never read as speech.
- **Bubbles:** the short version of the line only, around 8 words.
- **Silence beats:** 0:59, 1:11, 1:52, 2:02, 2:27, 2:42.
- **Thumbnail candidates:** Cole mid-fall through the glass; Bex looking up at the rope with six faces looking away.
