# LAST THREE / The Broadcast

2026-10-08. Paul selected Synty City Characters and authorized plan then build.

Three directions considered: (1) miniature mahogany-and-brass theatre, intimate but visually quiet; (2) bright toy competition, playful but less tense; (3) premium broadcast arena, sculptural concrete, black steel, luminous cyan water and warm amber safety markings. Build direction: The Broadcast, with the selected angular Synty contestants bringing dark comedy into a monumental arena.

Palette: ink #080e18, concrete #8e9ba8, ivory #f3efdf, amber #ffca72, aqua #73e3db. Each seat also has a consistent identifying colour, number and label. Typography: condensed heavy system sans for titles, monospace for broadcast metadata, readable sans for speech. Clear hierarchy, very little chrome.

Sets: five connected visual chapters. Glass suspended over a lit void; industrial press with huge pistons; circular flooded reservoir with a service deck; numbered mechanical trapdoors; a broken shrinking ledge. Lobby and chalk wall are quiet concrete galleries. Winners stand on a brass-accented podium.

Camera: three-quarter establishing shot; medium speaker shot; reverse reaction; death close-up. Composition changes by chapter and viewport. Cuts and transitions derive from event state. Reduced motion uses instant cuts and static poses. Event-local animation time resets on every seek; settled frames match replay and scrubbing.

Lighting: warm key, cool fill, visible practical light strips, soft contact shadows, fog, ACES tone mapping. Selective bloom belongs to practical lights, never washed-out character faces. No imported furniture realism mismatch. Original procedural architecture and props.

Characters: Ash/PunkGuy, Bex/GamerGirl, Cole/Jock, Dara/PunkGirl, Eli/HipsterGuy, Fenn/Paramedic, Gus/Hotdog, Hana/HipsterGirl. Rigged local GLB exports, palette atlas materials. Procedural bone performance supplies idle, stride, brace, shove, dodge, climb, reactions and celebration, with event-specific cartoon death staging. These are authored performances, not animations included by Synty.

Sound: voiceless synthesized bass pulse and filtered harmonics, per-stage pitches, spatial action hits. Starts only on explicit audio interaction; mute always available.

Tradeoff: prioritize expressive characters and legible obstacle state over expensive full-scene effects. Quality presets cap pixel ratio and shadows. Purchased source files remain private; local preview uses the real cast. Public fallback cast is explicitly a stand-in pending the release licence decision.
