## Recommended use

Keep 555/558 as the current composite reference. Use 596 as the strongest new whole-game alternative if the central theme is "help them, and they eliminate you." Use 579 if the ending needs a sharper confrontation with documented lies. Use 588 if the central relationship is one rope rescuer and the player that turns on it. These are alternative spines; their different deaths and finalist rosters cannot all be piled into one continuous game. Every borrowed sequence keeps its real speaker, target, action and consequence.

For suspense, show the mechanism before the decision: lever opens exit, holder gets a one-in-four dive; rope thrower becomes the floor anchor and must climb last on a later eligible turn; actual ballots open the named trapdoor. Faces, logos, save-debt links, reaction cuts and music should carry the explanation. No old powers, balance scores or random trapdoor lottery belong in these v5 scenes.
