# Last Three character replacement shortlist

Researched 2026-10-13. **Recommendation: KayKit's toy-like human characters**, customized into a consistent eight-contestant cast. Large heads, miniature bodies and simple faces create a larger stylistic change than replacing the current Synty POLYGON City cast with another adult-proportioned low-poly pack. This is an editorial judgment from actual publisher previews.

The publisher notes fewer Large-rig animations. Walks, movement and emotes are useful starting points; rope contact, lever holding, pleading and tape-specific falls still require fit checks or custom poses. A body rig is not proof of facial blendshapes or lip sync.

## Proposed cast treatment

| Element | Recommendation |
|---|---|
| Body | One coherent toy-like human family, with restrained size differences. |
| Identity | Eight distinguishable combinations of hair, head silhouette, glasses and clothing color. |
| Branding | Large chest logo and small sleeve origin flag, using the previously selected eight brands. |
| Clothing | Simple contemporary contestant tops and pants. Props must not imply mechanics absent from rules v5. |
| Performance | Simple materials and modest textures. Actual imported mesh/skin costs are not measured yet. |
| Reactions | Clear head, hand and body gestures. Facial controls must be checked rather than assumed from renders. |

Delight favors the KayKit proportions and face readability. Efficiency favors its source files and compatible animation library. Simplicity favors one shared visual family with branding. Performance needs an actual imported model check; publisher low-poly descriptions do not establish our runtime performance.

The next proposed implementation proof is one character: walk, pleading/reaction close-up, rope-hand pose and fall, with a chest logo and sleeve flag. Research is complete; this proof and a full cast replacement have not been executed.

## Other candidates screened

- [Quaternius Ultimate Modular Men](https://quaternius.com/packs/ultimatemodularcharacters.html): free CC0, 11 characters and 24 animations, modular parts. Its more adult proportions are close to the present style, so it is a weaker change for this brief.
- [Synty SIMPLE People](https://syntystore.com/products/simple-people-cartoon-assets): appealing blocky cartoon style, listed at $19.99 with 17 characters and three variations each. The [current Synty Store licence](https://syntystore.com/pages/one-time-purchase-licence) lists AI-related video/material restrictions and defines non-game productions as requiring written agreement. It is a visual reference rather than my recommended purchase for this project until applicable rights are clarified. This observation does not determine rights under an older purchase or a different store's licence.

Primary publisher pages, actual product screenshots, prices and advertised rig/file support were checked. The recommendation does not claim imported compatibility, animation quality, facial controls or final artistic acceptance.
